reversing a string using the 2-pointer method like two people standing at the two ends of a row and swapping places.

Suppose:

String = "HELLO"

Index:   0   1   2   3   4
         H   E   L   L   O
         ↑               ↑
       left             right
1. Put two pointers
left starts at the first character
right starts at the last character
left = 0
right = 4
2. Swap the two characters

Swap H and O:

O E L L H
↑       ↑
L       R

Then move both pointers toward the center:

left++   → 1
right--  → 3
3. Swap again

Now:

O E L L H
  ↑   ↑
  L   R

Swap E and L:

O L L E H

Move pointers:

left = 2
right = 2

Now both pointers have met, so we stop.

Final:

"OLLEH"
Why do we need only 2 pointers?

Because every swap puts two characters in their final positions.

For "HELLO":

H ↔ O    → O E L L H
E ↔ L    → O L L E H

The middle L doesn't need to move.

So instead of creating another string and copying characters, we work from both ends toward the middle.
